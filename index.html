How Voting Works

A transparent, fair, and professional election process.

🗳️

Transparent & Fair: The Instant-Runoff Process

Your rankings ensure the new AnderTech President represents the broadest possible support of our community. Your second and third rankings are just as vital to the roadmap as your first.

1
📊

The Initial Tally

We start by counting everyone's #1 Choice. If a candidate receives more than 50% of first-choice votes, they are declared the winner immediately.

↓
2
❌

The Elimination Round

If no one reaches the majority, the candidate with the fewest #1 votes is eliminated. This ensures that every single vote is working toward a consensus.

🔄
Vote Transfer
3
🔄

Vote Redistribution

If your first-choice candidate was eliminated, your vote isn't lost. It automatically transfers to your #2 Choice. This cycle repeats until one candidate achieves a majority of the remaining votes.

👑
🏆 Winner Declared

A candidate has achieved majority support and wins the election

Why this matters: This system ensures the new AnderTech President represents the broadest possible support of our community. Your second and third rankings are just as vital to the roadmap as your first.
